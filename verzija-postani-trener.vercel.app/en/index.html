Section 11

Frequently asked questions

Is this for beginners or for coaches who already work?

Both. The education is built so you can start from zero, and it will also lift your knowledge significantly if you already work as a coach. If you are a beginner you get a clear system and direction. If you already have clients you will learn how to sharpen your expertise, your clients’ results, your positioning and build a more serious coaching business.

How long do I have access?

Access is for life. Once you buy the education you keep access to every existing and future upgrade at no extra cost.

How much content do I get?

Right now more than 70 hours and more than 20 modules. Content is continuously extended with new lessons and materials.

Can I go through it at my own pace?

Yes. Every lecture is pre recorded and available 24 hours a day. You watch when it suits you and progress at your own pace.

Do I get support after buying?

Yes. Buying gives you access to a private Facebook group only for students of the education. You can ask questions there, share experience and get extra guidance as you develop.

Do I need a large following?

No. A large following is not a precondition for successful online coaching. It matters far more to attract the right people, build trust and get results with clients.

Do I have to clown around on social media?

No. The goal is not to become an influencer. The goal is to build the reputation of an expert people trust. Inside the education I teach you how to create content that attracts clients without clowning and without faking a life.

Will I learn how to reach online clients?

Yes. That is one of the key parts of the education. You will learn how to build a system that continuously attracts new enquiries and turns followers into clients.

Will I learn how to get better results with clients?

Yes. That is why the education includes experts from different fields. The goal is not only to have more clients, but to have satisfied clients who get results and stay with you long term.

What if I already have clients?

Great. Then you can use the education to raise your expertise, improve your working system, grow your client count and develop your personal brand.

Is the education only for online coaches?

Primarily yes. That said, a large part of the knowledge also applies if you run one to one or small group training in person.

How quickly can I expect results?

That depends on your level of engagement and application. Some students see first results very quickly, others need more time. What you get is a blueprint that can shorten your road to results significantly and help you avoid years of trial and error.

What if I currently have only a few clients?

That is exactly the situation a large number of our students started from. The education is built to help you grow a stable coaching business whether you start from 0, 2 or 10 clients.

Is this an education that teaches me only marketing?

No. Marketing is only one part of the equation. This education joins expert knowledge and business knowledge needed to build successful online coaching.

Who is this education not for?

Not for people looking for quick money without work. Not for people who are not ready to learn and apply. And not for coaches who do not want to work one to one with clients and develop a serious coaching business.

Why does this education exist?

Because I was a coach looking for answers myself. For years I built a coaching business through practice, mistakes, testing and work with more than 1000 clients. The goal of this education is to hand that blueprint to a new generation of coaches and help them reach results faster than I did.
